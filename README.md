# Reinforcement Learning in Motor Control of Musculoskeletal Systems

This repository contains the data and materials of our review
"Reinforcement learning in motor control of musculoskeletal systems".

## ⚠️ Corrections

**1. Figure 3E (figure panel).**
During figure assembly, an incorrect version of the Figure 3E panel was
inadvertently included in the submitted manuscript and in the corresponding
supplementary files. The correct version of this panel is provided in
[`figures/Figure_3E_corrected.png`](figures/Figure_3E_corrected.png). This
error occurred at the figure-assembly stage and does not affect the underlying
data, the analyses, or any of the reported results.

**2. Section 2.2.1 "Temporal trends of physics engines and learned dynamics
representations" (text).**
The sentence referencing the figure panels should read:

- Original (incorrect): "The annual frequencies and within-year proportions of
  these engine-use instances are shown in Figure 3E,F and Figure 3C,D,
  respectively."
- Corrected: "The annual frequencies and within-year proportions of these
  engine-use instances are shown in Figure 3C,E and Figure 3D,F, respectively."

We sincerely apologize for these errors. We have contacted the editorial
office; as the submission cannot currently be returned for revision at this
stage, we guarantee that these corrections will be applied at the proof stage
or in the next revision round.
